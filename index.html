    Bafoussam: a cool highland crossroads

    Bafoussam sits at roughly 1,400 metres above sea level, and you notice it immediately: cooler air, light jackets in the evening, and hills in every shade of green. As the capital of the West Region, it is busy and practical, but it is also the best base for exploring the Grassfields and the traditional chiefdoms of the Bamiléké people.

    Start at the central market in the morning, when it is liveliest. Then head to Bandjoun, where traditional architecture meets contemporary art. Foumban, with its Bamum palace and craft tradition, makes an easy day trip. The dry season, from about November to March, is the most comfortable time to travel.
